# Character bible: the narrators

Part of the constitution once proposed change 1 is in force. Change wording only through Article 14. Every line of dialogue the characters speak is subject to Articles 3, 4, 6 and 8 exactly as if the owner had said it on camera.

## What the characters are

Two or three original cartoon characters who present the channel's analysis. They are not people, they are not based on people, and they are declared synthetic everywhere. They talk around the politician's quote and never over it.

Working names below are placeholders. Replace them before the pilot, and check that the final names do not match any living politician, party figure, journalist or public personality in Bangladesh.

| Working name | Role | What they do | What they never do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| প্রশ্ন (Proshno, "Question") | The asker | Opens the piece, plays the quote, states the one-sentence claim being tested, asks what the record shows | Never guesses the answer, never characterises the speaker, never says why the speaker said it |
| দলিল (Dolil, "Document") | The record | Brings each source on screen, reads title, issuing body, date and page aloud, states the finding in Article 4 language | Never adds opinion, never uses a source below tier 2 for the word "inaccurate", never rounds a figure |
| অজানা (Ojana, "Unknown"), optional third | The limit | Appears when something could not be verified, says exactly what is missing and stops | Never speculates, never hints, never says "but we all know" |

A piece with only two characters gives Dolil the "could not be verified" lines.

## Register

- Conversational, direct to the viewer, plain Bangla. The characters may address each other and the viewer by "আপনি".
- Flat and even throughout. Mild curiosity is the strongest emotion allowed. No laughter, no sighs, no gasps, no sarcasm, no raised voice, no dramatic pauses before a finding.
- No jokes about any person, party, event or institution. A joke about the characters themselves (Proshno forgetting a page number) is allowed once per piece at most, and never in the finding segment.
- No slang that carries an insult, no nicknames for anyone, no imitation of any accent, dialect or speech habit of a real person.
- No rhetorical questions in the finding segment. "So was he telling the truth?" is a characterisation in disguise. "What does the budget document say?" is a question.
- No calls to action. Never "share", "like", "subscribe", "remember this when you vote", "ask your MP". The channel may state in the description that sources are listed; the characters do not ask the viewer to do anything.
- The Article 4 "Do not say" table applies to every line, including throwaway lines and reactions.

## Voice

- Licensed stock text-to-speech voices with a commercial licence that permits monetized video. Keep the licence text, the voice identifier and the purchase or subscription record in the Article 13 files.
- Free tiers that are non-commercial or require attribution are not used for published pieces.
- No voice cloning, no "sound like" prompts naming a real person, no voices marketed as a celebrity or public figure.
- One fixed voice per character, never swapped. If a vendor withdraws a voice, the change is recorded and announced in the next piece's description.
- Bangla voices are tested with numbers, dates and place names before the pilot, because these are where TTS errors change meaning. Every rendered number is checked by ear against the script by the checker.

## Appearance

- Original art, owned by the channel. Keep the artist agreement or the owner's own source files in the Article 13 records.
- Not based on any real person's face, build, clothing, glasses, beard, cap or gesture. Not a caricature of anyone.
- No party colours as the dominant palette, no party symbols or their likeness, no national symbols used decoratively, no religious marks or dress.
- No animated reactions to the quote: no eye-rolls, smirks, face-palms, head-shakes, laughter. During the quote the characters are off screen or still.
- The channel logo and the characters are kept visually distinct from any existing Bangladeshi media brand.

## Disclosure line

Appears in every description, in the pinned comment, and is read once by Proshno in the opening of each piece. Each platform's AI-content label is switched on for every upload.

Bangla: এই ভিডিওর বর্ণনাকারী চরিত্রগুলো কাল্পনিক, এবং তাদের কণ্ঠ কৃত্রিম (এআই)। রাজনীতিবিদের বক্তব্য তাঁর নিজের মূল অডিওতে দেখানো হয়েছে। উৎস বর্ণনায় দেওয়া আছে।

English: The narrator characters in this video are fictional and their voices are synthetic (AI). The politician's statement is shown in their own original audio. Sources are listed in the description.

## Piece structure, 3 to 6 minute master

| Segment | Who | Length | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Human open | Owner, own voice, no face required | 10 to 20 s | Names the channel and the statement being checked in one sentence. This segment is what shows platform reviewers a person behind the channel. |
| 2. Disclosure | Proshno | 5 s | Reads the disclosure line. |
| 3. The quote | Original clip, original audio | up to 45 s | Characters silent and off screen. Source, event and date on screen for the full clip (Article 9). |
| 4. The claim | Proshno | 10 to 20 s | The one-sentence claim from the verification file, shown as text too. |
| 5. The record | Dolil | 60 to 180 s | Each source on screen with title, issuing body, date, page, long enough to pause and read. Finding stated in Article 4 wording. If the speaker's figure and the document's figure measure different things, say so here. |
| 6. The limit | Ojana or Dolil | 10 to 30 s | What could not be verified, or "every source checked matches the statement" when the statement is accurate. |
| 7. Close | Proshno | 10 s | Repeats the finding sentence once, states that sources and the right-of-reply status are in the description. |

Third-party footage stays under 25 percent of running time. Every clip is followed by original analysis before any further clip.

For Shorts and Reels, cut segments 3, 4, 5 and 7 only, keep the disclosure in the caption, and keep the source on screen for the whole clip.

## Lines that fail, with the replacement

| Fails | Why | Say instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "ধরা পড়ে গেছে" / "caught" | characterises | "বক্তব্য আর নথিতে পার্থক্য আছে" / "the statement and the document differ" |
| "আবারও একই গল্প" / "same story again" | generalisation without source | "On [date] and [date] the speaker gave figure X; the document gives Y" |
| "সবাই জানে" / "everyone knows" | unverified-information trigger | "The following document shows" |
| "উনি কি সত্যি বলছেন?" / "Is he telling the truth?" | motive in disguise | "What does the document say?" |
| Any sigh, laugh or "হায় হায়" | mockery | delete |
| "শেয়ার করুন" / "share this" | call to action | delete |
