# Verify a statement yourself

Every video from দাবি ও দলিল (Claim and Document) is built so that a viewer can repeat the check without trusting the channel. This page explains how.

## What every video gives you

- **The exact quote**, in the speaker's own voice, with the event, the outlet or page it was captured from, and the date, shown on screen for the whole clip.
- **The one-sentence claim** that was tested, shown as text.
- **Each document** used, with its title, issuing body, date and page or section, shown on screen long enough to pause and read.
- **The finding**, in one sentence, and a note of anything that could not be verified.
- **The description** under the video repeats the quote source and every document with a link where one exists, and records whether the speaker's office was sent the finding before publication and what, if anything, they replied.

## How to repeat the check

1. **Go to the original clip.** Use the event, outlet and date shown on screen to find the full recording, and listen to the sentences before and after the quote. If the surrounding sentences change the meaning, report it to us.
2. **Open the document.** Use the title, issuing body, date and page from the video to find the document at its source. The usual sources are below. Read the page yourself rather than a news summary of it.
3. **Check that both measure the same thing.** A fiscal year is not a calendar year, taka are not dollars, gross is not net. The channel is required to say so when the speaker and the document measure different things, and not to call the statement inaccurate on that difference alone.
4. **Compare.** The channel's finding should be nothing more than the difference between what was said and what the document says. If you find a document the channel missed, or a reading error, report it.

## Where the documents live

| What the video cites | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| Budget speeches, budget documents, fiscal reports | Finance Division, Ministry of Finance: mof.gov.bd |
| Monetary, exchange-rate, reserve and banking figures | Bangladesh Bank: bb.org.bd |
| Population, labour, price and economic statistics | Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics: bbs.gov.bd |
| Election results, candidate affidavits, codes of conduct | Bangladesh Election Commission: ecs.gov.bd |
| Court judgments and orders | Supreme Court of Bangladesh: supremecourt.gov.bd |
| Parliamentary proceedings, questions and bills | Bangladesh Parliament: parliament.gov.bd, and Sangsad Television recordings |
| Gazette notifications | Bangladesh Government Press: dpp.gov.bd |
| Audit findings | Office of the Comptroller and Auditor General: cag.org.bd |
| The speaker's own earlier words | The speaker's official page or party channel, with the date given in the video |

If a cited document is not at its source any more, the channel keeps a saved copy with its capture date and will provide it on request.

## The rules the check follows

The constitution on this site sets the standard: a statement is called inaccurate only when a primary document contradicts it and at least two independent sources support the finding. The channel does not comment on anyone's character, motive, religion, family or private life, and applies the same test to every party and speaker.

## Report an error

If you find a mistake, use the error-report address in the About section of the channel's page on any platform. Every report is acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 72 hours, and every confirmed correction is listed on the corrections log on this site. The original video is not quietly edited or deleted.
